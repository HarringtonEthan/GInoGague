The math, in plain terms

Poisson distribution

Goals don't follow a bell curve — they're rare, semi-random events clustered around an average. The Poisson distribution is the standard way statisticians model exactly that, and it's the math behind every number above.

Likelihood

Add both teams' goals-for averages together and you get λ (lambda) — the expected combined total. The Poisson distribution spreads that average across every possible score, so the odds of finishing under or over any line can be added up directly.

The edge

Sportsbook odds imply their own probability. Edge is the model's probability minus the market's. Positive means the model sees more value than the price suggests — negative means the market's already ahead of it.
